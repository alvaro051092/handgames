# Content Strategy — handgames.app

**Date:** May 2026  
**Cadence:** 2–3 articles/week  
**Languages:** English (EN) · Spanish (ES) · Portuguese (PT)

---

## What we know about the site

handgames.app offers 5 online hand games — Chopsticks, Rock Paper Scissors, Morra, Odd or Even, and RPSLS — in three languages (ES/EN/PT), with no sign-up or download required. Each game page has a compact "How to play" section, but there is **no blog or article hub yet**. This is the biggest opportunity.

---

## Target audience

| Segment | Who they are | What they search |
|---|---|---|
| **Kids (8–14)** | Want to play immediately, share with friends, beat the CPU | "chopsticks game", "play RPS online" |
| **Parents** | Looking for offline or screen-light activities for kids | "hand games for kids", "games without materials" |
| **Educators** | Need low-prep classroom or camp games | "playground games no equipment", "finger games classroom" |
| **Spanish/Portuguese speakers** | Under-served market for this type of content | "palillos juego", "piedra papel tijeras online" |

## Competitor landscape

| Site | Games | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| bloob.io | RPS | Multiplayer online | Single game only |
| rpsgame.org | RPS | Strong SEO for "RPS online" | No other games |
| playrps.online | RPS | Clean UX | No content/blog |
| rpsls.net | RPSLS | Good for RPSLS keyword | Very narrow |
| Game portals (CrazyGames, SilverGames) | Many | Domain authority | Generic, not hand-game focused |

**Key insight:** No competitor owns the "hand games" niche as a whole. Almost all compete only on RPS. **handgames.app is the only site that clusters all classic hand games together** — this is the topical authority angle to exploit.

## Internal linking plan

| RPS history/strategy | `/rps/en/` |
| RPSLS article | `/rpsls/en/` |
| Morra article | `/morra/en/` |
| Odd or Even article | `/odd-even/en/` |
| "Best hand games for kids" | All 5 game pages |
| "Hand games at school" | Chopsticks + RPS + Odd or Even |
| Strategy articles | Link back to rules article + game page |

Cross-link between articles in the same cluster:
- Chopsticks rules ↔ Chopsticks strategy ↔ Chopsticks variations
- RPS history ↔ RPS strategy ↔ RPSLS rules
- "Best hand games" ↔ individual game articles

## Blog structure recommendation

Add a `/blog/` (EN), `/blog/` under `/es/` (ES), and `/blog/` under `/pt/` (PT).

Each article should follow this template:
1. **Intro** — hook + CTA to play the game (within first 2 paragraphs)
2. **Main content** — rules, history, strategy, etc.
3. **Play now** — embedded or prominent link to the game
4. **Related articles** — 2–3 internal links
5. **FAQ schema** — 3–5 questions for rich snippets
