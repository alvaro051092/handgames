How to play Chopsticks (the finger hand game)

Chopsticks is a two-player finger game, also known as Sticks or finger chess. Each player has two hands that start with one finger up, and on every turn you tap one of your hands against one of your opponent's to add fingers to it. Here you can play Chopsticks online for free against the AI (easy, medium or hard), against a friend on the same device, or watch the CPU play both sides — no sign-up needed.

How it works

  • Each player starts with 1 finger on each hand
  • On your turn, tap one of your hands against one of your opponent's hands
  • Your fingers are added to theirs — a hand that reaches 5 or more is out (it counts as 0)
  • Eliminate both of your opponent's hands to win

The split (classic rule)

Many versions also let you spend a turn redistributing fingers between your own two hands, as long as the new arrangement is different. The online game here uses the basic tap-only rules, with no split, so every turn is a tap — quick to learn and quick to play. Want the full split rules and when to use them? Read our complete Chopsticks guide: rules, strategy and variants.

Objective

Force both of your opponent's hands to 5 or more fingers. A hand that reaches 5 is out and counts as 0; when both are out, you win. Play a single game or best of 3.

Strategy tips

  • Check for a kill every turn. Before every tap, see whether any of your hands plus any of theirs makes 5 or more, then check what the move leaves you exposed to.
  • Watch your 4s. A hand with 4 fingers is out as soon as any opposing hand taps it. A hand with 1 finger is the safest: only a 4 can finish it.
  • Don't feed their big hand. Tapping adds your fingers to their hand, which can make it strong enough to eliminate one of yours next turn. Check the total you hand over before you tap.
  • One hand down, half the threats. Once a hand is eliminated your opponent can only attack with the other one, which makes their next moves easy to predict.

Playing Chopsticks vs the AI

On Easy the CPU plays random moves; on Medium and Hard it eliminates one of your hands whenever it can and otherwise plays at random. A fun fact: with these tap-only rules the game is solved — the player who moves second can always force a win in at most 8 turns in total. Since you move first against the AI, you need it to slip, so keep your hands out of reach and punish every mistake.

About the game

Chopsticks (also called finger chess or sticks) is a hand game played worldwide in schoolyards and playgrounds. Unlike Rock Paper Scissors, it's a pure strategy game — no luck involved. Both players have complete information at all times.

What is the split in Chopsticks?

How do you win at Chopsticks?

Is there a winning strategy in Chopsticks?

With the tap-only rules used here (both hands start at 1 finger and 5 or more eliminates a hand), the player who moves second can always force a win within 8 turns in total, so the first player needs the opponent to make a mistake.
