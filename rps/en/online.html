How to play Rock Paper Scissors with a friend online

  1. Create a room. Enter your name and tap “Create new room” to get a 5-character code.
  2. Invite your friend. Send the link with the “Invite a friend” button, or share the code so they can use “Join with a code”.
  3. Pick and reveal. Each of you chooses in secret, with a click or with your camera (hold the gesture for 3 seconds). Once you have both picked, the result appears and you keep going with “Next round”.

No sign-up and nothing to install. The room expires after about an hour without activity. Prefer to play solo? Try Rock Paper Scissors against the CPU or read how camera mode works.
