How to play Rock Paper Scissors

Rock Paper Scissors (known as Jan-Ken-Pō in Japan) is one of the most popular hand games in the world. Here you can play online for free against the CPU, with a friend online or with your phone's camera. Two players pick one of three gestures at the same time: the one that beats the other wins the round.

The 3 gestures

  • 🪨 Rock — closed fist
  • 📄 Paper — open hand
  • ✂️ Scissors — two fingers extended

The rules

  • 🪨 Rock crushes ✂️ Scissors
  • ✂️ Scissors cuts 📄 Paper
  • 📄 Paper covers 🪨 Rock

Objective

Both players reveal their gesture simultaneously. The winning gesture earns one point. In best of 3, the first to win 2 rounds takes the match and tied rounds score nothing; in a single game, a tie ends the match.

Strategy

Each gesture beats one and loses to one — pure reads and psychology. No dominant option exists: every choice is a calculated risk.

A few common patterns to look for: winners tend to repeat the gesture that just won — throw what beats it. Losers tend to switch — predict the switch. If both players have repeated the same gesture twice, neither wants to throw it a third time.

History

Its exact origin is uncertain: secondary sources trace its ancestry to a Chinese hand game called shoushiling, linked to the Han Dynasty (206 BC – 220 AD). It is believed to have reached Japan in the 17th century under the name jan-ken, and the West in the early 20th century. Today it's one of the most recognized hand games on the planet — used to settle disputes, break ties, and fill waiting rooms.

Variants

The most popular expansion is Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock — adds Lizard and Spock to create 10 possible outcomes per round, cutting tie probability nearly in half. Popularized by The Big Bang Theory. Play RPSLS →

Play Rock Paper Scissors with a friend online

Is your friend far away? Skip the video call and the installs: play Rock Paper Scissors with a friend online in a private room, each on your own phone or computer and with no account.

  • 1. Create a room. Enter your name and tap “Create new room” to get a 5-character code.
  • 2. Invite your friend. Send the link with the “Invite a friend” button, or share the code so they can use “Join with a code”.
  • 3. Pick and reveal. Each of you chooses in secret; once you have both picked, the round is revealed. A tie scores nothing, so you simply play another round.

The score keeps adding up and there is no round limit, so play as long as you like. Each of you can choose with a tap or with your camera (see camera mode below). Rooms are for two players and expire after about an hour without activity, so share the code only with your friend.

Camera mode: Rock Paper Scissors with your real hand

In camera mode you don't press any buttons: you show your hand to the camera and the game reads the gesture. Choose “Camera (beta)” when you set up a game against the CPU or in an online room with a friend.

  • Allow the camera. Your browser asks for permission. If you refuse, or there is no camera, the game falls back to clicks.
  • Show a gesture. Rock is a closed fist; Paper, an open hand with four fingers extended; Scissors, index and middle fingers extended with the others folded. Only one hand is read.
  • Hold it for 3 seconds. A 3-2-1 countdown confirms your move. If you change gesture, it starts over.

For best results use good lighting, a plain background and keep your whole hand inside the guide oval. On phones the game asks for the rear camera by default; on a computer it uses your webcam. The first time, the hand-recognition model takes a few seconds to load. Recognition runs in your browser: the video is not sent to our servers, and in online rooms your friend only receives the name of your move.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Rock Paper Scissors?

Rock crushes Scissors, Scissors cuts Paper, Paper covers Rock. Both players reveal a gesture simultaneously — the winning gesture scores a point. First to reach the target score wins.

Is Rock Paper Scissors free to play online?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up or download required. Play directly in your browser on any device.

What game modes are available?

Four modes: vs CPU (you against the computer), online room (play a friend from anywhere), 2 Players (two people, one device), and Battle (CPU picks for both players — pure luck). In vs CPU and online rooms you can choose with clicks or with your camera.

What is the best strategy for Rock Paper Scissors?

No gesture dominates — every choice beats one and loses to one. Watch your opponent's patterns: many players repeat their favorite gesture or unconsciously echo the winning one. No strategy guarantees a win, but reading your opponent helps.

Where did Rock Paper Scissors originate?

Its exact origin is uncertain. Secondary sources trace its ancestry to a Chinese hand game called 'shoushiling', linked to the Han Dynasty (206 BC – 220 AD). It is believed to have reached Japan in the 17th century as 'jan-ken' and spread to Western countries in the early 20th century.

What is Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock?

Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock is an expanded version with 5 gestures: Rock, Paper, Scissors, Lizard, and Spock. Each gesture beats two others and loses to two others, reducing the chance of a tie. Popularized by The Big Bang Theory.

Can you play Rock Paper Scissors with a friend online without signing up?

Yes. Create a room, send your friend the invite link or the 5-character code and play in near real time. There is no account and nothing to install, and the room expires after about an hour without activity.

Can you play Rock Paper Scissors with your camera?

Yes. Choose Camera (beta) in vs CPU mode or in an online room, allow camera access and show a closed fist for Rock, an open hand for Paper, or your index and middle fingers for Scissors. Hold the gesture for 3 seconds to confirm it. If the camera is unavailable you can keep playing with clicks.

Is my camera video sent to a server when I play?

No video is sent to our servers. Your hand is recognized in your browser (the recognition model is downloaded from a public CDN when you turn the camera on). In online rooms, our server only receives your name, a random session identifier, the room code and your move (rock, paper or scissors); the video and the photo of your gesture are never sent.
